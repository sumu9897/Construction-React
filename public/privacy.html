Privacy Policy · PMCC Owner Portal

Effective: August 2026 · PMCC S.A.R.L. (Project Management and Construction Company), Al-Bustan Building, Amaret Chalhoub, Nahr El Mot Highway, Metn, Lebanon.

What the app is

The PMCC application ("the Portal") is a private service for clients who hold a purchase contract with PMCC and for PMCC staff. It is not open to the public; access is granted by PMCC on a per-contract basis.

Information we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address, used to sign you in and to link you to your residence.
  • Project data: information related to your purchase: your unit, payment schedule and status, selections, approved changes, questions you send us, and documents we share with you.
  • Usage data: when you last opened the Portal and which shared updates you viewed, kept as part of the project record.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than files you deliberately upload), or any advertising identifiers.

How it is used

Solely to administer your purchase and construction of your residence: sharing progress, schedules, payment status, documents, and answering your questions. Questions to the in-app assistant are processed by an AI service using only the project information already shared with you. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (hosted in the European Union, Frankfurt) and protected by per-account access rules: an owner can only ever access records tied to their own unit.

Your choices

You may request a copy or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to info@pmcclb.com. Data forming part of the contractual record (payments, approvals, correspondence) may be retained as required for legal and contractual purposes.
